A fully interactive 3D Rubik's Cube, built as a way to practice cube-solving
algorithms without carrying a physical cube around. It supports standard move
notation (`R U R' U'`), a scramble/reset/undo history, and a library of seeded
algorithms across the CFOP stages with adjustable-speed playback.

Try it on the [Rubik's Cube page](/rubiks).
