title: "Sunday Morning Pancakes"
slug: "sunday-pancakes"
date: 2026-07-19
author: "Ana Oliveira"
excerpt: "The pancake recipe that shows up every slow Sunday, scaled for a hungry house."
category: "recipe"
mealType: "breakfast"
ingredients:
  - "2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "2 tbsp sugar"
  - "2 tsp baking powder"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "1 3/4 cups milk"
  - "1/4 cup melted butter, plus more for the pan"
steps:
  - "Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl."
  - "In a second bowl, beat the eggs, then whisk in the milk and melted butter."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir just until combined — a few lumps are fine."
  - "Heat a buttered pan over medium heat and pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake."
  - "Flip once bubbles form and hold their shape on the surface, about 2 minutes per side."
  - "Serve warm, stacked, with whatever's in the fridge that wants pouring on top."
---

This is the one that gets made on the mornings nobody's in a rush. It scales up
easily if there are more hands at the table than usual — just double everything
and keep the pan going.

No strong opinions on toppings here. Syrup, fruit, a spoonful of yogurt — whatever's around.
